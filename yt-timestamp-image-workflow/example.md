# Example

## Thumbnail Candidate Example

For a topic/title like:

```text
Why is Singapore so rich?
```

Expected thumbnail output format:

```markdown
### Thumbnail Candidate 1 — Tiny Island, Huge Money
Title/Text on thumbnail: "HOW RICH?"
Visual concept: A tiny Singapore island sitting on top of a giant pile of money, with a shocked stickman pointing at it.
Composition: Big yellow text at the top, tiny island and oversized money pile in the center, shocked character on one side.
Colors: Yellow headline with black outline, green money, red/blue accent shapes, clean white or light background.
Why it works: It creates curiosity immediately by contrasting Singapore’s tiny size with huge wealth.
Generation prompt: Create a horizontal 16:9 YouTube thumbnail inspired by the Zenn channel thumbnail style. Video topic/title: Why is Singapore so rich? Thumbnail concept: tiny island on a giant pile of money. Text on thumbnail: "HOW RICH?" Simple bold cartoon/doodle illustration, thick black outlines, high contrast, minimal detail, flat colors, clean uncluttered background, large central subject, readable on mobile, curiosity-driven. Big yellow uppercase text with thick black outline. Avoid photorealism, 3D, anime, glossy corporate vector art, clutter, tiny text, long sentences, unreadable words.
Filename: thumbnail_01.png
```

### 001 — 00:00 — starting youtube is easy
Prompt:
Create a 16:9 landscape YouTube visual in intentionally bad Microsoft Paint style.

Scene timestamp: 00:00
Scene idea: Everyone thinks starting YouTube is easy.

Draw a crude stick figure in a blue shirt smiling confidently beside a huge red YouTube play button. The stick figure is holding a sign that says “EASY!” while a tiny laptop sits on a simple desk. Make it look stupidly optimistic and low-effort, with wobbly black lines, flat bright colors, and a plain white background.
