# Style Guide — Beginner MS Paint Timestamp Images

## Default Style

Create extremely simple beginner drawings that look like they were made quickly in Microsoft Paint by someone who is not good at drawing.

The drawings should feel:
- Amateur
- Funny
- Simple
- Intentionally bad
- Childish
- Quickly hand-drawn
- Clear and readable

## Canvas

- Aspect ratio: **16:9 horizontal landscape**.
- Suitable for YouTube video frames.
- Not vertical.
- Not square.
- White background by default.
- Mostly empty white space.
- Keep the main subject centered and readable.
- Do not crop important objects.
- Leave enough space around characters and objects.

## Line Style

- Thick, uneven black outlines.
- Wobbly hand-drawn lines.
- Simple black line drawings.
- No clean professional vector lines.
- No polished illustration finish.

## Characters

Characters should be stick figures:
- Round heads.
- Line bodies.
- Line arms and legs.
- Dot eyes or circle eyes.
- Very basic facial expressions.
- Simple mouths: smile, frown, shocked open mouth, confused line mouth.

Recurring default:
- Main character/narrator: simple stickman, optionally blue shirt.
- Problem/enemy/obstacle: red or gray object/monster/sign.

## Objects

Draw objects with basic shapes only:
- Squares
- Circles
- Rectangles
- Arrows
- Simple tables
- Boxes
- Trees
- Rooms
- Signs
- Screens
- Stickmen
- Question marks
- Simple symbols

## Colors

Use flat colors only.

Allowed/common colors:
- Black outlines
- White background
- Green
- Brown
- Gray
- Red
- Yellow
- Orange
- Blue

Use red arrows or red question marks when needed.

## Text Inside Images

Text is allowed only when it helps explain the idea.

Rules:
- Keep it short.
- Make it easy to read.
- Spell it correctly.
- Prefer 1–3 words.
- Use simple handwritten-looking text.
- Never show the script timestamp/timecode in the image. The timestamp is metadata only.
- Do not add corner time labels like “0:41”, “00:41”, “1:05”, frame counters, subtitles, captions, watermarks, or UI overlays.

Good examples:
- “WHY?”
- “0 VIEWS”
- “HELP”
- “MONEY”
- “ALGORITHM”

Avoid long paragraphs or tiny text.

Bad examples:
- “0:41”
- “00:41”
- “1:05”
- Any timestamp/timecode copied from the prompt.

## Quality Level

Use **medium quality** generation.

Do not use high quality unless Naufal explicitly asks.

## What Not To Do

Do NOT make it:
- Photorealistic
- Realistic human
- Cinematic
- 3D
- Disney style
- Anime style
- Realistic cartoon style
- Professional vector art
- Glossy
- Modern UI design
- Highly detailed
- Complex textured
- Beautiful or premium

The entire point is that the drawings look simple, funny, and beginner-made.
